Build an interactive educational web app (using Python and Streamlit) called "Velouria: The Compositional Guide".
This app is an interactive, visual reference guide for composers and producers to learn and construct music in the Velouria genre. It does not create or export MIDI files; instead, it provides musical notation, interactive piano rolls, theory breakdowns, sound-design guidelines, and arrangement recipes.
1. Header & Official Dictionary Definition
At the top of the app (or in an expandable hero banner), include the dictionary entry:
Ve·lou·ri·a /vəˈlʊər.i.ə/ noun
Definition: A genre of romantic, cinematic ambient and chamber-inspired electronic music characterized by tender melodic fragments, suspended harmonic movement, and textures softened by distance, memory, or environmental space.
Etymology: Coined from velour (a plush, soft-woven fabric) and aetheria / -ia (suggesting an atmospheric or dreamlike realm).
Related Forms:
Velouric (adj.): Exhibiting subtle qualities, traces, or underlying textures characteristic of Velouria.
Velourian (adj./n.): Directly embodying the aesthetic and compositional principles of Velouria.
2. Core Navigation: 5 Layer-by-Layer Modules
Structure the app into five distinct instructional tabs or chapters:
Module 1: Harmonic Movement & Dyads (The Foundation)
Concept: Explain Harmonic Suspension and how parallel 4th-to-3rd sighing dyads create emotional consolation without resolving into predictable pop/classical cadences.
Interactive Transposition Guide: A key selector (all 12 chromatic keys) that recalculates and displays:
Symmetrical Base Progression:
Pair 1 (over Root 5th): [P4 → M3] (e.g. in A♭: [A♭3+E♭3] → [G3+E♭3])
Pair 2 (over Step-Up Root): [P4 → M3] (e.g. [B♭3+F3] → [A3+F3])
8-Bar Expanded Progression:
Im(add9) (Bars 1–2) ➔ ♭VImaj7 (Bars 3–4) ➔ ♭IImaj7 (Bars 5–6) ➔ IVm9 / V7sus4 (Bars 7–8).
Notation & Piano Roll Display:
Standard music staff notation (rendered using VexFlow or ABCjs in HTML/SVG).
An interactive, stylized Piano Roll grid showing pitch rows, bars, and hold lengths.
Module 2: The Sincere Fragment (Melody & Phrasing)
Concept: Explain the principle of the short, 2-to-3 note descending motif and the power of silence between phrases.
Melodic Formula:
Step 1: The Longing (Question / tension over minor chord)
Step 2: The Ache (Step-down half-step)
Step 3: The Consolation (Landing on the warm major 3rd of the shifted chord)
Visual Piano Roll: Display the right-hand notes over time, with clear annotations marking where intentional empty bars occur.
Module 3: Variations & Transformations Catalog
Provide visual notation, piano roll views, and musical explanations for the key variations:
01 · Ghost Note: Skipping Beat 1 to start mid-thought.
02 · Octave Dream: Dropping the melody and harmony down one octave for intimate weight.
03 · Ringing Tail / Residue: Holding the final note across bar lines into granular space.
04 · Warm Centre: Landing directly on the major 3rd and arpeggiating upward.
05 · Twin Peaks Lift: Swapping the dream chord for a bright major chord before melting back into minor.
06 · Dorian Sigh: Resolving downward to the 6th/2nd for wistful ambivalence.
07 · Late Breath: Shifting melodic attacks half a beat late (Beat 1½) for human hesitation.
08 · Drone Pedal: Anchoring the entire 8 bars with a single stationary open fifth in the bass.
Module 4: Mediated Distance & Sound Design
Provide concrete recipes for treating instruments:
Piano Layer: Soft felt tone (e.g. Pianoteq / Kawai), velocities between 40–60, warm close mic placement with dark room decay.
String / Pad Swell Layer: Slow attack (600–900 ms), 24 dB low-pass filter, subtle chorus/ensemble, detuned saws or vintage polysynth (Voyetra / MiniFreak).
Granular Residue & Tape Weathering: Using granular emitters (SoundMorph Dust) or BBD delay freeze (u-he Colour Copy) strictly on the tail ends of phrases rather than across the whole mix.
Bass Layer: Rounded triangle/sine wave filtered below 300–500 Hz to act as an emotional floor rather than a rhythmic groove.
Module 5: Environmental Setting & Space
Concept: How room tone, ventilation hum, quiet nighttime rain, and distant field recordings establish an ambiguous physical location.
Mixing Matrix: A visual signal-flow diagram showing how foreground piano, mid-ground string swells, background granular tails, and room tone occupy distinct depths and frequency slots.
3. Visual & Technical Implementation Details
No MIDI Creation or Audio Generation: Keep the app focused on being a pure instructional manual and composition handbook.
Visuals: Use HTML/CSS/Canvas or SVG to draw clear, elegant piano-roll representations and sheet music.
Styling: Design a dark, refined UI (charcoal, deep navy, velvet-gold accents, and clean typography) fitting the Velourian aesthetic.
